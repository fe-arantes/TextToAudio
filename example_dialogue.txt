It was a busy Saturday afternoon at the shopping mall.
Sarah: Hi! I'm looking for a jacket for the winter. Can you help me?
Clerk: Of course! Are you thinking of something casual or more formal?
Sarah: Something casual, but that I could also wear to work.
Clerk: We have this navy blue one on sale. Would you like to try it on?
Sarah: It fits perfectly! How much is it?
Clerk: It's forty dollars with the discount.
Sarah: Great, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Clerk: Sure! I'll meet you at the register.
Sarah left the store happy with her brand new jacket.
